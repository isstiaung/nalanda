# Features

What Nalanda does, one page per area, accurate to the code on `main`. Each page links the runbook
that operates the feature and the decision ([ARCH.md §16](../../ARCH.md#16-decision-log)) that shaped
it. The short version is the [README](../../README.md); the privacy rules behind every page are in
[docs/privacy.md](../privacy.md).

- [Cataloguing](cataloguing.md) — scan a barcode or search a name and the shelf fills itself: providers, covers, formats and editions, language, series, where it lives, what you paid, a record's pressing and condition, and fields of your own.
- [Reading](reading.md) — every read is yours, with pages, re-reads, ratings and reviews, quotes, goals, a year in review, and the two pages that find more to read.
- [Shelves and search](shelves-and-search.md) — the filter bar and its sorts, saved views, tags, bulk edit, full-text search with operators, and pages for creators and publishers.
- [Imports and exports](imports-and-exports.md) — Goodreads, StoryGraph, LibraryThing and libib in; a CSV out that carries every field back; backups.
- [Sharing](sharing.md) — a public link per view, gift lists, feeds, QR codes, and what never appears on a share page.
- [Lending](lending.md) — loans with due dates and history, and what you've borrowed from someone.
- [Connections](connections.md) — two households that both run Nalanda follow, comment, borrow and recommend, pairwise and off by default.
- [Members and privacy](members-and-privacy.md) — admins and members, display names and the switches, the interface language (हिन्दी and தமிழ் drafts, your own translation), sessions, a read-only API, item history, the trash.
- [On your phone](on-your-phone.md) — install it to the home screen, scan with no signal, or scan a whole shelf in one go, with nothing of yours kept on the phone.
